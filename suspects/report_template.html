How to use this report
  1. Start at the top: faces are ordered by how likely they are wrong and how much damage they may do.
  2. For each card, compare the cropped face with the faces below it (“Compare with similar faces”) and decide.
  3. To find it in digiKam, open the album folder shown on the card, open the file, and look for the face described under “Where”. The full photo shows where the face is.
  4. If it is wrong, correct it in digiKam as usual. If it is right, tick “Reviewed” (stored in this browser only).
  5. This tool never changes digiKam or your photos. Re-run it after you have corrected tags.
