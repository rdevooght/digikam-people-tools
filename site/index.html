A LITTLE CONTEXT

Behind the numbers

What counts as an appearance?

One photo per person, regardless of how many face regions are recorded. Non-person tags are excluded. A group means the intersection: every selected person must be tagged in the same photo.

Two ways to read the percentage

Tagged photos: matching photos ÷ all photos with at least one included person tag in that period.

Person tags: selected people’s tag occurrences ÷ all included person-tag occurrences in that period. For a group, only photos containing everyone contribute; each selected person contributes one occurrence.

Periods with no denominator have no percentage, rather than a misleading 0%.

Dates & companions

Timelines use the photo’s recorded capture date, falling back to its album date by default. digiKam does not store when a tag was added in this schema. Undated photos still count in totals and companion rankings, but not in timelines. Companion rankings and the three headline stats cover all dates; the year selector filters the timeline only.

Bring your own collection

From the project folder, with Python 3.10 or newer:

python extract.py /path/to/digikam4.db

Then reload site/index.html. No Python packages, image files, or internet connection are needed. The database is opened read-only. For a custom People tag:

python extract.py /path/to/digikam4.db --list-roots
python extract.py /path/to/digikam4.db --people-root 42

See README.md for all options. Exporting replaces the sample data.
